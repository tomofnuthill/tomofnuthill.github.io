This website is dedicated to my interests and the various projects and experiments I have conducted in their pursuit. The vast majority of these fall under the catagories of math, physics, and chemistry.

Simulating Poker (Texas Holdem) , May 2024

In May of 2024 me and my good friend Ronny built a computer program to find the change of a known poker hand winning a game, assuming that nothing was known about the hands of the opponents. It works by creating a large quantiy of random games (usually over 1 000 000) and finding the fraction of those games that the hand being tested wins.

Synthesis of Azides

In February of 2021 I began the synthesis of sodium azide as inspired by Leonid Lerner's Small-Scale Synthesis of Laboratory Reagents. In October of 2022 I finally succeded after several synthetic redesigns.

Math IA

For my IB math class in grade 12 I wrote this math paper. It's about taking the Fourier transforms of tidal charts. I wrtote a computer program in python to do this and ran it with 5 years of minutely tide data from Vancouver. I then linked the results to gravitational effects on the earth caused by the moon. There were frequencies which could be attributed to the moon's orbital period, its declination, and many other phenomena.
